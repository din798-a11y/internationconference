ABOUT THE CONFERENCE

The International Conference on Literature, Social Sciences, Regional Studies and Management Practices (LSRM-2026) is scheduled for September 25–26, 2026, operating in a Hybrid (Online & Offline) mode. Organized by IQAC, VNSB Ltd Arts & Commerce College, Vadnagar and Shri V. R. Patel College of Commerce, Mehsana, and affiliated with Hemchandracharya North Gujarat University, Patan, this conference focuses on the theme "Indian Knowledge System".

It aims to foster research from diverse cultural backgrounds, welcoming participants from educators, scholars, industrialists, policymakers, and industry. It also seeks to facilitate meaningful discussions and the higher education, in alignment with NEP 2020's emphasis on collaborative research for innovative solutions to contemporary global challenges.

AUTHOR GUIDELINES

  • CONTENT
    Papers should be presented on research purpose, methodology, results, and references.
  • FORMAT
    MS Word, 12 pt font, Times New Roman, 1.5 line spacing, including figures, tables, etc.
  • FONT
    12 pt font, 1.5 spacing. Times New Roman for English, Shruti for Gujarati, Mangal for Hindi.
  • SUBMISSION
    Soft copy of the paper in MS Word to accvad2026@gmail.com
  • AUTHORSHIP
    A maximum of one author per paper is allowed for participation. The last page of the research paper must be the conclusion and references.
